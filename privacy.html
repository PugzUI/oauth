Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

This OAuth client is used only by the project owner to authorize personal tools (such as rclone) to access the owner’s own Google account data (for example Google Drive).

  • No third-party users are invited.
  • Tokens stay on the owner’s machines.
  • No analytics, ads, or data sale.
  • Google account data is handled under Google’s terms for the APIs the owner enables.

Contact: the Google account used as the OAuth support email for this Cloud project.
